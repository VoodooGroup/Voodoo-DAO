DAO Info & How to Move Funds

Key Parameters:

  • Minimum to propose: 10,000 VDO
  • Quorum: 10% of total supply
  • Majority: Yes votes ≥ 51% of total votes cast
  • Timelock: 2 days delay after passing
  • Only whitelisted addresses can be called

How to transfer funds:

  1. Connect your wallet (PulseChain)
  2. Approve VDO for the DAO
  3. Select "Normal Proposal"
  4. Target = treasury or other whitelisted address
  5. Calldata = encoded call — use abi.hashex.org
  6. Submit → Vote → Queue → Execute (after timelock)
